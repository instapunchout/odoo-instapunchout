Punchout for your Odoo webshop

Let your B2B buyers shop on your Odoo website from inside their procurement system, and return the cart for approval.

This module is free, the InstaPunchout service is not

This module only connects Odoo to the InstaPunchout platform and does nothing on its own. To use it you need an InstaPunchout subscription, billed directly by InstaPunchout (not through Odoo Apps):

  • a one-time setup fee, and
  • a yearly license per buyer connection.

Contact info@instapunchout.com or visit instapunchout.com for a quote before installing.

How it works

  1. Your buyer clicks your shop in their procurement system (for example SAP Ariba, Coupa or SAP via OCI).
  2. InstaPunchout validates the request and the buyer is logged in to your Odoo website as a portal user.
  3. They browse your catalog with their own prices and fill the cart as usual.
  4. The cart is sent back to the procurement system for approval, in cXML or OCI format.

What you get

  • cXML and OCI punchout on your existing Odoo eCommerce
  • Automatic login, with optional creation of portal users under the buyer's company
  • Cart edit: buyers can reopen a cart from their procurement system
  • Multi-website and multi-company support
  • Optional with InstaPunchout: receiving purchase orders into Odoo and sending invoices electronically

Requirements

  • Odoo 17 or later with the eCommerce app, on Odoo.sh or on-premise. Third-party modules can't be installed on Odoo Online: contact us for the options there.
  • An InstaPunchout subscription (see above). We configure each buyer connection with you.

Data and external services

To work, this module communicates with the InstaPunchout service at punchout.cloud:

  • Punchout start: the headers, query string and body of requests to /punchout are sent to InstaPunchout, which decides whether to log the buyer in. It can create portal users on your database and log buyers in to your website.
  • Cart return: the punchout cart is sent to InstaPunchout (cart and partner IDs, products, quantities, prices, taxes, totals, currency, delivery date and note).
  • Script: during a punchout session only, the page loads a JavaScript file from InstaPunchout (punchout.js) that adds the return-cart button. Visitors outside a punchout session don't load it.

The module collects no usage statistics. See our Privacy Policy.
